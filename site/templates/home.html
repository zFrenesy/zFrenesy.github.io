Open problems, shared research.

An open archive of research attempts, partial results and reviews across mathematics, economics and computer science. Pick a question, read what has been tried, and add to it.

An open workspace, not peer review. Many attempts are LLM-generated or LLM-assisted and may contain errors, invalid proofs, invented references or false claims. Automated screening does not establish correctness, novelty or validity. Scrutiny and reproduction are welcome; develop useful work into papers for proper peer review, and respect credit, citation, licences and third-party rights. About

Contribute in three steps

  1. Choose a question and read its statement and earlier attempts.
  2. Work on it yourself, with a chat assistant, or with a coding agent such as Codex or Claude Code.
  3. Submit through a form or a pull request, with an honest claim and full credit for people and tools.
